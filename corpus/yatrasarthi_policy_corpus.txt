YatraSarthi Travel Policy & Passenger Rights Corpus
====================================================

SECTION 1: DGCA PASSENGER RIGHTS (India)

Under DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements (CAR) Section 3, Series M, Part IV:

1.1 Flight Delay Compensation
- Delay of 2-4 hours: Airlines must offer meal vouchers and refreshments.
- Delay of more than 4 hours: Passenger may opt for full refund with no cancellation penalty.
- Delay of more than 6 hours (for flights scheduled between 8PM and 3AM): Airline must provide hotel accommodation.
- Overnight delay: Airline is responsible for hotel + transfer costs.
- No compensation is payable if delay is due to extraordinary circumstances (severe weather, ATC, security).

1.2 Flight Cancellation Rights
- Cancellation notified < 2 weeks before departure: Full refund OR re-routing at no extra cost.
- Cancellation notified < 24 hours before departure: Full refund + compensation of 200% of basic fare (max INR 10,000) for flights <1500 km.
- Cancellation due to force majeure (natural disaster, extreme weather): Only full refund owed, no additional compensation.
- Right to re-route: Passenger can demand re-routing at the earliest opportunity at no additional cost.

1.3 Denied Boarding (Overbooking)
- Involuntary bumping on domestic flights: Compensation = 200% of basic fare up to INR 10,000.
- Airline must first ask for volunteers before involuntarily bumping passengers.

SECTION 2: TRAIN (IRCTC/Indian Railways) CANCELLATION POLICY

2.1 Refund Rules on Cancellation
- Cancellation >48 hours before departure: 25% of fare deducted (minimum clerkage of INR 90).
- Cancellation 48-12 hours before: 50% of fare deducted.
- Cancellation <12 hours / no-show: No refund (PRS tickets).
- TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt): Filed for train delay >3 hours, train not running, or train terminating before destination. Refund processed within 90 days.

2.2 Train Delay Policy
- Delay 2-4 hours: No automatic compensation; TDR can be filed for refund.
- Delay >4 hours: Full refund claimable via TDR if passenger does not travel.
- Delay due to floods/disasters: IRCTC grants full refund without penalty.

SECTION 3: HOTEL CANCELLATION POLICIES

3.1 Standard Hotel Policy
- Free cancellation: Most hotels allow free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in.
- Late cancellation (<24 hours): One-night charge typically applied.
- No-show: Full stay may be charged.
- Force majeure: Hotels with a written policy must refund in full for natural disasters or government travel bans.

3.2 Non-Refundable Rates
- Non-refundable bookings: No refund regardless of reason.
- Exception: Many hotels now offer full credit (not refund) for COVID-related cancellations.

SECTION 4: YATRASARTHI RECOVERY RANKING LOGIC

4.1 How YatraSarthi Ranks Recovery Options
YatraSarthi generates up to 4 distinct recovery plans when a disruption is detected:

Plan ranking criteria (in order of priority):
1. Minimum total additional cost to traveller
2. Minimum additional time added to itinerary
3. Maximum confidence score (feasibility of the alternative)
4. Preservation of trip purpose (e.g., does not move hotel out of destination city)

4.2 What "Amber" Status Means
- A node is marked AMBER (at-risk) when its incoming buffer time is being consumed by an upstream delay, but has not yet been fully consumed.
- Amber means: the connection is still possible but tight — if the upstream delay increases by more than the remaining buffer, this node will break.
- Example: Flight lands 45 minutes late. Hotel check-in is 60 minutes away. Buffer = 15 minutes. Status = AMBER.

4.3 What "Red" Status Means
- A node is marked RED (broken) when the buffer has been fully consumed and the connection is impossible at current delay.
- Recovery is required: the system will generate alternatives for the broken node and all downstream nodes.
- Hard constraints (e.g., non-refundable same-day flights) break immediately regardless of buffer.

4.4 Cascade Propagation
- When a node breaks, its delay propagates downstream through all connected edges.
- Each edge has a bufferMin field: the minutes of slack available before the next node is impacted.
- Soft constraints can absorb delay up to bufferMin. Hard constraints break immediately.

SECTION 5: WEATHER-IMPACT RULES

5.1 Rainfall Impact
- Rainfall 0-10mm/hr: No impact on flights or trains. Road travel adds 10-15 min delay.
- Rainfall 10-30mm/hr: Moderate — flight delays likely (30-60 min). Road travel severely slow (+45-90 min).
- Rainfall >30mm/hr: Severe — flight cancellations likely. Trains may be delayed 2-4 hours. Roads may be impassable.
- Rainfall >50mm/hr: Extreme — airports issue ground stops. NDRF deployed for road rescue.

5.2 Wind Speed Impact
- Wind <40 km/h: No aviation impact.
- Wind 40-60 km/h: Caution for small aircraft. No wide-body impact.
- Wind >60 km/h: Diversions possible. Ground operations slowed.
- Wind >80 km/h: Airport may declare emergency. Large-scale cancellations.

5.3 Visibility Impact
- Visibility >5 km: Normal operations.
- Visibility 1-5 km: Reduced visibility procedures; delays 30-90 min.
- Visibility <1 km: Cat II/III ILS required. Many airports in India do not have Cat III — cancellations likely.
- Visibility <200m: Airport closes to all traffic.

SECTION 6: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Can I get compensation if my flight was delayed due to heavy rain?
A: Weather is considered a force majeure event under DGCA rules. Airlines owe you meal vouchers for delays over 2 hours, and a full refund for delays over 4 hours, but no monetary compensation beyond the ticket price.

Q: My train was delayed 5 hours due to flooding. Can I get a refund?
A: Yes. File a TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) within 72 hours. Since the delay was weather-related, IRCTC typically grants a full refund without the usual deduction. Processing takes up to 90 days.

Q: Why is one recovery option cheaper than another?
A: YatraSarthi ranks options by total additional cost first. A same-day replacement train is usually cheaper than a replacement flight on the same route. YatraSarthi also checks if your original booking qualifies for a weather-related waiver, which can make rebooking free.

Q: What does the confidence score mean in the recovery options?
A: Confidence reflects how likely the alternative is to be available and executable at the time of booking. A 95% confidence option is available and confirmed. An 80% confidence option may require waiting for seat release or is on a route with historically high occupancy.

Q: My hotel checkout is fine but my cab to the airport is now at risk. What does YatraSarthi do?
A: YatraSarthi detects that the cab node is amber (buffer nearly consumed). It will proactively suggest departing 45 minutes earlier or booking a higher-reliability cab type (metered > app-based in heavy rain), and flag the change for your confirm tap before anything is booked.

Q: Is a non-refundable hotel stay covered if there is a natural disaster?
A: Not automatically. However, most OTAs (MakeMyTrip, Booking.com) have a force majeure policy that grants a credit note or full refund for bookings in declared disaster zones. YatraSarthi will surface this option and draft the waiver request for you.

Q: What is triggerSource in the YatraSarthi system?
A: Every disruption in YatraSarthi is tagged with a triggerSource field indicating what caused it: vendor_cancellation (airline or hotel directly cancelled), weather (storm, flood, extreme rain), or user_action (traveller voluntarily changed a booking). The triggerSource determines which compensation rules apply and which recovery paths are surfaced first.
